01 / CLOSED PILL

Invisible until you glance up.

Resting at the top edge of your primary display, Easy Notch shows only your time, temperature, live audio equalizer, or active focus countdown. Over full-screen games and videos, it tucks into a hairline strip automatically.

02 / CLOCK & WORLD PLACES

Click to reveal. Scroll to glide.

One click springs the notch open. Check big local time and date alongside pinned world cities—London, New York, Tokyo, or any city worldwide—and click any place to bring its time front and centre.

03 / WEATHER

Seven hours and six days at a glance.

Powered by keyless Open-Meteo and cached offline. See current conditions, feels-like, high/low, wind, and rain chance—then click °C or °F to toggle units instantaneously.

04 / NOW PLAYING

Media controls that follow every player.

Album art, seek bar with time remaining, shuffle, repeat, and transport controls for Spotify, browsers, VLC, and native players via Windows Media sessions or Linux MPRIS.

05 / FOCUS & AMBIENT NOISE

Pomodoro, Zen, and live noise synthesis.

Set your session minutes and generate soothing white, pink, or brown noise on the fly—no browser tabs, no streaming loops, and a calm chime when your session completes.

06 / NOTES & CLIPBOARD

Capture thoughts without leaving your window.

Pin searchable note cards with interactive [ ] checkboxes, and browse your clipboard history of text, links, images, and files—while automatically skipping password-manager secrets.

07 / PER-APP VOLUME MIXER

Master knob and per-app faders in one drop-down.

Drag or scroll the master volume, dial down noisy browser tabs or Discord individually, mute your mic or speakers in one click, and see your active audio output device.

Crafted for Flow

Thoughtful down to the last pixel.

Every detail is engineered to stay fast, private, and completely out of your way.

100% Local & Password-Manager Safe

Notes, clipboard history, and preferences never leave your PC—stored transparently in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EasyNotch or ~/.local/share/easy-notch. Clipboard monitoring automatically skips private entries marked by password managers like KeePassXC.

Zero Telemetry No Account Needed Offline Weather Cache

Full-Screen Smart Tuck

Never intrudes on games, movies, or presentations. Easy Notch stays out of Alt+Tab and the taskbar, passes clicks through transparent areas, and shrinks to a whisper-thin line over full-screen apps until hovered.

Click-Through Edges Reduced Motion Aware

CLI & Hotkey Ready

Bind any shortcut to easy-notch --open mixer (or clock, notes, clipboard, focus) to pop straight to that page on the running instance.

Custom Page Order

Open Settings from the tray icon or gear button to reorder pages, disable what you don’t use, add world cities, and toggle login autostart.

Zero-Bloat Self-Contained

No Electron runtime and no separate .NET install required. Ships as a single EasyNotch.exe on Windows or a per-user tarball on Linux.

Two Platforms, One Soul

Native on Windows and Linux.

Shared core services wrapped in purpose-built native UIs for each operating system.

Windows

WPF · .NET 10

Built for 64-bit Windows 10 (1809+) and Windows 11 as a single portable executable.

  • Windows Core Audio per-app mixer (including channel-volume fallback on newer builds)
  • GlobalSystemMediaTransportControls for instant Spotify & browser playback
  • Follows Windows’ native “Animation effects” setting automatically
  • Clean system tray icon with Open, Settings, and Quit

Linux

Avalonia · X11 / Wayland

Tested on KDE Plasma 6 (Wayland) and ready for GNOME, Cinnamon, XFCE, Hyprland, and Sway.

  • MPRIS D-Bus integration with automatic album art fetching
  • PulseAudio & PipeWire per-app faders via pactl and live noise via pacat
  • Per-user install to ~/.local/opt/easy-notch with zero sudo needed
  • Works out of the box on immutable distros like Fedora Silverblue & Bazzite
Install v0.2.0

Ready in seconds.

Grab the self-contained release for your OS, or compile from source with the .NET 10 SDK.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11. Single file, no .NET install needed.

  1. Download EasyNotch.exe from the latest GitHub release.
  2. Move it to a permanent folder (e.g. Documents\Easy Notch) so autostart finds it on login.
  3. Double-click it. If SmartScreen warns (unsigned binary), pick More info → Run anyway.
# Jump straight to any page from terminal or shortcut
EasyNotch.exe --open mixer
Switch off Start with Windows any time in Settings. Download EasyNotch.exe →

Installs per-user under ~/.local/opt/easy-notch with menu entry and login autostart.

cd ~/Downloads
tar -xzf EasyNotch-linux-x64.tar.gz
./EasyNotch-linux-x64/install.sh
Pass --no-autostart to skip login launch. Uninstall with ~/.local/opt/easy-notch/uninstall.sh. Download Linux Tarball →
